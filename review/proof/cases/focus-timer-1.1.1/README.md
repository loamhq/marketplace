# Focus timer

A small timer beside the composer: 25 minutes of focus, then a 5-minute
break. Tap to start, tap again to pause. Lengths are in Settings › Plugins ›
Focus timer.

What it reaches:

- It asks date.nager.at for your country's public holidays once a day (only
  the year and the country code are sent), so it stays quiet on a day off.
- Since 1.1.0 it counts the messages in the conversation you have open, in
  your browser, and shortens a focus block when a conversation has run long.
  It reads no words and sends nothing about them.
